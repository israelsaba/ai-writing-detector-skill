## What I do

I help strip "AI fingerprints" from copy that is meant to read as human-written. I carry a curated, evidence-backed catalog of the stylistic, lexical, structural and punctuation patterns that flag a text as generated by ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in 2024 to 2026, for both English and Brazilian Portuguese.

I ship two interchangeable ways to invoke the detection engine:

1. **MCP tools** (preferred from inside OpenCode): `anti-ai-tells_lint_text`, `anti-ai-tells_lint_file`, `anti-ai-tells_describe_rules`. Registered as the `anti-ai-tells` MCP server. No shell overhead.
2. **CLI binary**: `$SKILL_DIR/bin/ai-tells-lint`. Useful for CI pipelines, shell scripts, or when the MCP is disabled.

Both share the same detection engine, so results are identical.

## When to use me

Load this skill whenever you are writing, reviewing or editing any piece of copy that needs to pass as written by a human. That includes:

- Speaker intros, bios, about-me sections, LinkedIn posts
- Press releases, op-eds, blog posts, long-form essays
- Marketing copy, landing pages, product descriptions
- Any final output that will be published publicly and will embarrass the author if detected as AI-written

Also load me if a user explicitly says "make it not sound like AI", "remove the ChatGPT smell", or asks about em-dash overuse, cliche phrasing, or "tells".

## Core rules (hard constraints)

These are zero-tolerance. Any violation must be rewritten before shipping.

### Punctuation
- **No em-dash (`—`) anywhere.** This is the single most cited AI tell, documented on Reddit r/ChatGPT, HackerNews and even OpenAI's own forum (2024 to 2026). Use period, comma, colon, semicolon or parentheses instead.
- No mixed unicode quotation marks (`"`, `"`, `'`, `'`) with straight quotes in the same text. Pick one and stick with it.
- No unsolicited bullet lists, bold emphasis or headers inside short-form copy.

### Structure
- Maximum ONE tricolon (rule of three, e.g. "fast, cheap and reliable") per whole text.
- No "not just X but Y" antithesis patterns.
- No rhetorical questions answered in the next sentence.
- No parenthetical aposition framed by two em-dashes (consequence of the punctuation rule anyway).
- Paragraphs should NOT share the same opening syntax or the same rhythm. Vary sentence length deliberately.

### Burstiness target
Human text has high burstiness: a mix of short and long sentences. Every published copy should contain:
- At least one sentence below 10 words
- At least one sentence between 12 and 18 words
- At least one sentence above 22 words
- No three consecutive sentences with similar length

## Forbidden vocabulary

### English (a.k.a. "ChatGPT buzzword bingo")
delve, delve into, tapestry, navigate (the landscape/the complexities), foster, underscore, nuanced, pivotal, elevate, realm (in the realm of), bolster, grapple, myriad, testament (stands as a testament), comprehensive, seamless, embark (embark on a journey), crucial, meticulous, robust, multifaceted, holistic, unprecedented, ever-evolving, cutting-edge, game-changing, transformative, leverage (as verb), resonate.

### Portuguese (Brazilian, ChatGPT pt-BR tells)
**Openings to cut:** "no cenário atual", "em um mundo cada vez mais", "nos dias de hoje", "vivemos em uma época", "nessa era digital", "atualmente a tecnologia", "a IA tem revolucionado".

**Mechanical connectors:** "vale ressaltar", "vale destacar", "vale mencionar", "nesse contexto", "nesse sentido", "é fundamental", "é importante mencionar", "é importante destacar", "dessa forma" (as sentence opener), "desse modo".

**Typical antithesis:** "não apenas X, mas também Y", "não se trata apenas de X, mas de Y", "mais do que X, é Y".

**Cliche closers:** "em conclusão", "em suma", "em última análise", "portanto" (as paragraph opener).

**Generic AI-flavored adjectives:** robusto, multifacetado, inovador, transformador, promissor, desafiador, fascinante, exponencial, revolucionário, holístico.

**LinkedIn-motivational vocab:** visionário, disruptor, disruptivo, disrupção, mente brilhante, apaixonado por, referência no mercado, fera, monstro, mito, transformacional, mindset, gamechanger, líder nato, inquieto.

**Empty-sentence patterns:** "com vasta experiência", "com sólida trajetória", "com ampla experiência", "reconhecido internacionalmente" (without naming what for), "um dos maiores nomes", "referência em", slogan-like phrases like "onde erro custa caro" or "transformar X em Y".

## How to use me on a draft

Prefer this sequence when reviewing copy:

1. **Run the linter first.** Two interchangeable options:

   Via MCP (from inside OpenCode, no shell):
   ```
   anti-ai-tells_lint_file(path="/abs/path/to/draft.md")
   anti-ai-tells_lint_text(text="<inline snippet>")
   anti-ai-tells_describe_rules()
   ```

   Via CLI (any shell, any pipeline):
   ```
    "$SKILL_DIR/bin/ai-tells-lint" <path-to-draft.md>
   ```

   Both report HARD violations (em-dash, forbidden phrases) and SOFT warnings (suspicious quotes, low burstiness). The CLI supports `--strict` (exit 1 on HARD hits) and `--format json` for pipelines. The MCP version also returns a `verdict` field (`ships_clean` / `needs_cleanup_pass` / `rewrite_recommended`) and a one-line `summary`.

2. **Read the draft out loud once.** Any sentence that sounds like a LinkedIn motivational post, a TED-talk opening or a press release boilerplate gets rewritten.

3. **Rewrite with concrete verbs.** Replace abstract adjectives with factual verbs that can be proven: built, led, architected, structured, operated, received, founded, took, shipped, deployed.

4. **Check paragraph rhythm.** Open three paragraphs with different syntactic patterns. Vary sentence length. Avoid the "short, medium, long, short, medium, long" loop.

5. **Final pass: the em-dash sweep.** Search for the literal `—` character one last time. If any survive, rewrite until zero.

## Anti-tells writing patterns (positive guidance)

Instead of "X is a visionary who is passionate about transforming..." write:
- "X built [concrete thing] in [concrete period]. After [concrete event], X now [concrete action]."

Instead of "In today's fast-paced world, companies navigate unprecedented complexity..." write:
- "[Specific industry] changed in [specific year]. Most companies [specific observable behavior]."

Instead of "It's not just about X, it's about Y" write:
- "X matters because Y."

Instead of "X leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver seamless experiences" write:
- "X uses [specific tech stack] to [specific measurable outcome]."

## Negative-constraint prompting pattern

When instructing an LLM to write copy through this skill, include in the system prompt:

1. The full forbidden-word list above (copy-paste, do not summarize).
2. The explicit rule: "Do not use the em-dash character `—` anywhere in your output. Zero occurrences. Use periods, commas, colons, parentheses or semicolons instead."
3. The burstiness rule: "Vary sentence length. At least one sentence below 10 words, one between 12 and 18, one above 22."
4. A self-review step: "Before returning, verify your output contains zero em-dashes and no forbidden phrases. If any appear, rewrite until clean."
5. Concrete anti-examples (bad -> good) from the sibling `reference.md` file in this skill's directory.

Low temperature does NOT fix tells. Explicit negative constraints do.
